# Texture generation prompts

Method: built-in `image_gen`; opaque raster material maps. No fal requests or paid API calls were made. Original PNGs are preserved under local `output/cabin-v7/textures`; the workspace public assets are lossless WebP conversions, with no repainting or creative editing in conversion.

## nomex-quilt.webp

Create ONE perfectly seamless tileable square PBR BASE COLOR / ALBEDO texture of real aerospace off-white Nomex insulation fabric. Orthographic flat scan, fills entire square edge to edge, no perspective, no objects, no borders, no labels, absolutely NO directional lighting or cast shadows. Photographic micro detail of tightly woven ivory-white synthetic cloth fibers, subtle warm grey yarn variation, tiny natural wrinkles and faint abrasion from careful use, matte textile not metal, not plastic. SMALL diamond quilt stitching repeated in a regular 8 by 8 grid over the full image, extremely fine grey stitching thread, understated puckering near the stitch lines. Very neutral warm ivory overall, no strongly dark weathering, no deep gouges. This is a texture for physical 3D space station wall insulation and astronaut suit textile, not a picture of a room. Produce a high resolution square diffuse/albedo map with consistent diffuse value and tileable opposite edges.

## apple-skin.webp

One seamless PBR diffuse albedo color texture map for the skin of a real ripe dark red Braeburn apple. Flat orthographic unwrapped equirectangular surface, 2:1 wide image, fills whole frame. No apple silhouette, no object, no background, no shadows, no highlights, no lights, no perspective: this is ONLY the fruit skin surface flattened into a rectangular UV texture. Extremely fine real skin pores, tiny muted pale yellow lenticels scattered irregularly, organic deep crimson red mottling with restrained golden orange thin irregular longitudinal streaks running vertically from top edge to bottom edge, more red than yellow, very detailed authentic macro fruit photography, matte diffuse light. Delicate natural variations, not stripes drawn with paint, not metallic. Opposite left and right edges seamlessly tile. Avoid broad yellow bands, avoid blurry AI smearing. High resolution.

## pressure-suit-fabric.webp

One seamless tileable square photographic PBR diffuse albedo texture of real off-white white spacecraft EVA pressure garment fabric. Flat orthographic macro scan of slightly crumpled, unquilted tightly woven white nylon/Nomex with extremely fine weave, subtle soft irregular creases, barely visible beige-gray thread wear, tiny seam-free surface wrinkles. This is a square material texture, NOT a suit or a room: cloth fills entire frame edge to edge. No diamond quilting, no grid, no seams, no objects, no text, no shadowing, no lighting gradient, no perspective, no strong grime. Uniform diffuse lighting, natural neutral ivory-white hue, realistic textile micro details, seamless opposite edges.
